How it works

Scan → Analyze → Propose → Risk-check → Trade

The AI can only ever propose a trade. Deterministic code decides whether a proposal is allowed — and nothing executes without your explicit approval.

01

Local AI analyzes

An open-source model on your phone reads market intelligence — RSI, MACD, EMAs computed outside the LLM — and drafts a structured proposal.

02

Risk Engine verifies

Pure, deterministic code runs 12 checks — position size, daily loss limits, stop-loss requirement and more. No hallucinations can become orders.

03

You approve

See exactly what the AI wants to do and why it was allowed. One tap to approve or reject — the default is always human-in-the-loop.

04

Paper fills first

Every account starts with ₹10,00,000 in virtual cash and fills at live Coinbase prices. Live trading is a separate, explicit, approval-gated mode.

Features

Built to be your copilot — not your pilot

Everything a serious trading companion needs, with trust designed into the core.

Local AI Model Hub

The app probes your RAM, storage and CPU, then recommends the right open-source model — 3B for speed, 7B for depth. New models discovered via a registry.

Market Intelligence

RSI, MACD, EMA/SMA, VWAP, ATR and Bollinger Bands are computed in plain code — outside the LLM — then fed to the model as structured context.

Backtesting

Test what following the Copilot's signals would have done over history: return, max drawdown, win rate and profit factor — before risking anything.

Deterministic Risk Engine

Position limits, daily-loss circuit breakers, duplicate-order protection, a kill switch. If a check fails, the order cannot exist — and you see why.

AI Trading Journal

Every trade records why the AI suggested it, why you accepted, the fill and the result — so the copilot can teach you about your own patterns.

Offline Mode

No internet? The local model, your portfolio, strategies and journal still work. Live prices and execution reconnect when you do.

The trust boundary

No hallucination can become an order

The LLM's only output is a validated, structured TradeProposal. Everything after that point is deterministic code the user can audit. That is the single most important rule of Trader Copilot — and it is enforced by architecture, not promises.

AI confidence scores are always shown as assessments, never win probabilities. Live trading stays locked until a real broker pack is deliberately connected.
